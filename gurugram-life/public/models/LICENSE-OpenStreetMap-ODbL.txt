realcity.json.gz: roads, building outlines and land use of central Gurugram, derived from OpenStreetMap.
Map data (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0:

This derived database (realcity.json.gz) is likewise made available under the ODbL. It is built by
scripts/realmap/build_realmap.py from the Haryana extract at download.openstreetmap.fr.
Building heights not recorded in OpenStreetMap are estimated by the build script.
