# Getting other sites to link to gurugramlife.com

Links from other sites are one of the biggest things Google looks at, and no setting on our side can replace them. Post these yourself (with your own accounts), one or two a day, not all at once. Always say you made the game: undisclosed self-promotion gets posts removed and accounts banned.

Link to a specific page when it fits (for example `https://gurugramlife.com/places/iffco-chowk` in a traffic thread), not only the homepage.

## 1. Reddit (read each subreddit's rules first; some allow self-promotion only on certain days)

>

You can walk or drive around Cyber City, Golf Course Road, Sohna Road, Sadar Bazaar and 20 other real areas, do office shifts, race on Golf Course Road, wrestle a pehlwan at Leisure Valley, and survive the monsoon floods (there's a boat). It's free, runs on phone or laptop, and works in English, Hindi or Haryanvi.
>
> https://gurugramlife.com
>
> I'd love to hear which Gurugram things I should add next. Which chowk deserves its own traffic event?

**r/playmygame** or **r/IndieGaming** (follow their feedback-post format)

## 2. Game directories (each gives a permanent link)

- **itch.io**: create a free "HTML" project page named *Gurugram Life*, with genre Simulation, tags (open-world, india, multiplayer, browser, life-sim), 4–5 screenshots from the reels, and a "Play now at gurugramlife.com" link in the description. itch.io pages rank well on Google on their own.
- **IndieDB**: add the game with the same text and screenshots.
- **Product Hunt** (optional, one day only): launch with the 20-second reel as the video.

## 3. Local blogs, pages and groups

- Gurugram and Haryana Facebook groups, plus Instagram meme pages: share the daily reel and put the link in the comments or bio.
- Local news and lifestyle sites that write about Gurugram: send a short email.

> Subject: A free video game set in Gurugram (made in Gurugram)
>
> Hi, I've made a free 3D game set in real Gurugram: Cyber City, Golf Course Road, Iffco Chowk jams, monsoon waterlogging, baraats and all. It plays in the browser in English, Hindi or Haryanvi. I thought your readers might enjoy a funny local angle. Happy to share screenshots, reels or a short note on how it was made. https://gurugramlife.com

- College tech clubs and gaming societies in Gurugram and Delhi NCR: ask them to try it and share it.

## 4. Your own channels

- Put `https://gurugramlife.com` in the Instagram bio, the YouTube channel description and every Short's description (the daily texts already include it).
- Pin a comment with the link under each YouTube Short.

## What not to do

- Don't buy links or use "SEO link packages". Google penalises them.
- Don't post the same text in many groups on the same day. Spread it out and change the wording.
